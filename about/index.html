About · Est. 2016

A neighborhood shop with big-city standards.

Hone & Harbor started in 2016 with two chairs, one hot-towel cabinet and a promise: nobody leaves until they're happy with what they see in the mirror.

Nine years later we have four barbers, a coffee machine that never rests and the same promise. We cut every hair type, from first haircuts to silver foxes, and we still believe a good barbershop belongs to its neighborhood — somewhere to slow down, talk, and walk out sharper.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is free. Weekday mornings are your best bet. Booking online guarantees your time.

How long does a haircut take?

Most haircuts take 45 minutes including the consultation and hot-towel finish. Combos run 60–80 minutes.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Absolutely. Kids 12 and under get the Kids Cut at a lower price — we have a booster seat and plenty of patience.

Can I choose my barber?

Of course. Pick your barber when booking, or choose 'Any barber' for the earliest available time.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes — in the shop or by phone, in any amount. They never expire.

Where do I park?

Free 2-hour street parking on King Street, plus a public garage at 34 St Philip St, two minutes' walk away.
